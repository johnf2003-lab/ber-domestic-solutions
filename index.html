Services

Assessments and advice for every stage of a home's life

From a straight-up certificate for a sale or letting, to a full advisory report ahead of a retrofit, every assessment is carried out independently and submitted directly to the SEAI National BER Register.

BER for sale & rental

A valid BER certificate is a legal requirement before you can advertise a home for sale or rent. A site visit is arranged at a time that suits you, and your certificate — together with the accompanying advisory report — is issued and published on the SEAI register, usually within a few working days. Legally required for listing

New dwelling BER

Provisional BER assessments from design-stage drawings, followed by a Final BER once the build is complete, snagged and ready for handover. Works with self-builders, builders and architects to keep the paperwork moving alongside the build programme. Provisional & final certificates

SEAI grant & retrofit advisory

A pre-works BER assessment establishes your home's starting point and the post-works assessment confirms the improvement — both are needed to draw down SEAI grants for insulation, heat pumps and other upgrades. The advisory report sets out which measures will move the rating furthest. Pre & post-works assessments
